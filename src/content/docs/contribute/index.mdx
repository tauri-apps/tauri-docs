We, the maintainers, are really excited that you are interested in contributing to Tauri. Before submitting your contribution though, please make sure to take a moment and read through the [Code of Conduct](/contribute/coc/), as well as the appropriate section for the contribution you intend to make:

- [Issue Reporting Guidelines](/contribute/issue-reporting/)
- [Pull Request Guidelines](/contribute/pr/)
- [Documentation Guidelines](/contribute/doc-guide/)
- [Development Guide](/contribute/dev-guide/)
